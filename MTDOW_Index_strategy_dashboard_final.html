Core Performance Metrics
MetricDefinitionFormulaGood Value
Win RatePercentage of total trades that are profitable(Winning trades ÷ Total trades) × 100> 45%
Profit FactorRatio of total gross profit to total gross loss. Shows how many rupees you earn per rupee lost. A PF of 2.0 means you make ₹2 for every ₹1 you lose.Sum(Winning P&L) ÷ |Sum(Losing P&L)|> 1.5
ExpectancyAverage P&L per trade after all costs. Tells you what you can expect to earn per trade on average over many trades.Total Net P&L ÷ Total Trades> 0
Avg WinnerAverage gross profit on winning trades (before costs)Sum(Winning P&L) ÷ Count(Winners)—
Avg LoserAverage gross loss on losing trades (before costs). Should be smaller than avg winner for a healthy R:R.Sum(Losing P&L) ÷ Count(Losers)—
Risk:Reward (R:R)Ratio of average loser to average winner. R:R of 1:3 means you risk ₹1 to make ₹3. Higher is better but usually comes with lower win rate.|Avg Loser| : Avg Winner> 1:2
Risk-Adjusted Returns
MetricDefinitionFormulaGood Value
Sharpe RatioMeasures return per unit of risk. Calculated on daily P&L (not per-trade). A Sharpe of 2.0 means the strategy earns 2x its daily volatility annualised. Higher = more consistent returns relative to risk taken. Most hedge funds target > 1.0.(Daily Mean P&L ÷ Daily Std Dev) × √252> 1.5
Calmar RatioAnnual return divided by maximum drawdown. Measures how well the strategy recovers from worst-case losses. Higher = faster recovery from drawdowns.Annual P&L ÷ |Max Drawdown|> 2.0
Max DrawdownLargest peak-to-trough decline in cumulative P&L. Represents the worst losing streak you would have experienced. This is the "pain" number — can you sit through this loss?Lowest (Cumulative P&L − Peak P&L)Lower is better
Avg DrawdownAverage of all drawdown periods. Shows typical underwater experience, not just worst case.Mean of all drawdown values—
Max Consec. WinsLongest streak of consecutive winning trades. Useful for position sizing — don't over-leverage during streaks.——
Max Consec. LossesLongest streak of consecutive losing trades. The mental test — can you keep trading through 8 losses in a row?—< 10
Trade Analysis Metrics
MetricDefinitionFormulaGood Value
MFE (Max Favourable Excursion)Maximum profit a trade reached before it was closed. If MFE = 80 pts but exit = 50 pts, you "left 30 pts on the table". Used to evaluate trail efficiency.Peak unrealised profit during trade—
MFE Capture RatePercentage of MFE actually captured at exit. 85% capture means you exited near the peak. 50% means your trailing SL was too wide or you held too long.(Actual P&L ÷ MFE) × 100> 70%
MAE (Max Adverse Excursion)Maximum drawdown a trade experienced before recovering. Large MAE on winners means your SL is correctly placed — letting trades breathe.Peak unrealised loss during trade—
Avg DurationAverage time a trade is held open. Shorter = faster capital turnover. Intraday strategies typically 30-120 minutes.Mean(Exit Time − Entry Time)—
Best / Worst TradeLargest single winning and losing trade. The worst trade should ideally be smaller than 2× the average loser — no fat tail blow-ups.——
Cost Metrics
MetricDefinitionFormulaGood Value
Gross P&LTotal profit/loss before any transaction costs. This is "what the strategy made" in pure price action terms.Sum of all (Exit − Entry) for each trade—
Net P&LGross P&L minus all costs (brokerage + taxes + slippage). This is "what you actually take home".Gross P&L − Total Costs> 0
BrokerageBroker commission per trade. Zerodha charges ₹20 per executed order. Synthetic futures = 4 orders = ₹80 per trade.₹20 × 4 orders ÷ Lot Value—
Taxes (STT + GST + Exchange)Securities Transaction Tax on sell side, GST on brokerage, exchange transaction charges, SEBI charges, stamp duty. These scale with trade value — larger positions = higher taxes.Zerodha calculator values—
SlippageDifference between expected entry/exit price and actual fill price. In liquid options (ATM Nifty/BN), slippage is near zero for 1 lot. For 10+ lots, expect 1-3 pts negative slippage.Expected Price − Actual Fill~0 for 1 lot
Cost DragPercentage of gross profits consumed by costs. If cost drag = 15%, you lose 15% of your alpha to friction. Lower is better — scale with more lots to reduce per-lot impact.(Total Costs ÷ Gross P&L) × 100< 20%
Walk-Forward Validation Metrics
MetricDefinitionFormulaGood Value
In-Sample (IS)Data the strategy was developed/optimised on. Good IS results don't guarantee future performance — they just show the strategy found patterns in past data.——
Out-of-Sample (OOS)Unseen data used to validate. If OOS results are positive, the strategy generalises to new data. This is the "real" test.—> 0
OOS EfficiencyHow well OOS performance matches IS. 100% means OOS performed exactly as IS predicted. >100% means OOS was even better. <50% suggests overfitting.(OOS Annual P&L ÷ IS Annual P&L) × 100> 50%
Walk-ForwardMultiple overlapping IS→OOS windows tested sequentially. If most OOS windows are profitable, the strategy is robust across different market regimes. Our test: 8 windows, 2016-2025.—7/8+ windows profitable
Anchored ModeIS window keeps expanding (always starts from 2016). More data = more stable parameter estimation. Used in our backtest.——
Strategy-Specific Terms
TermDefinition
SPH / SPLSmall Pivot High / Low — local swing high/low confirmed by 2 subsequent bars. SPH = bar's high is highest of surrounding bars (with 2-bar confirmation). Price action structure building block.
LPH / LPLLarge Pivot High / Low — formed when SPH exceeds previous SPH (LPH), or SPL breaks below previous SPL (LPL). These are the key support/resistance levels the strategy trades.
L1 / S1Primary entry signals. L1 = Long when price breaks above LPH (close above LPH). S1 = Short when price breaks below LPL (close below LPL). Entry at next bar's open.
Phase 1/2 TrailTrailing stop follows SPL (for longs) or SPH (for shorts). SL ratchets up as new higher SPLs form. Never moves against the trade direction.
Phase 3 TrailAggressive trailing at previous bar's low (longs) or high (shorts). Activates when profit reaches 0.5% of entry price. Tighter trail = locks in more profit but exits sooner.
Synthetic FuturesReplicating a futures position using options: Long = Buy ATM CE + Sell ATM PE. Short = Sell ATM CE + Buy ATM PE. Lower margin than futures, similar P&L profile.
EOD CloseAll positions squared off at 3:15 PM. No overnight risk. Strategy is purely intraday.
